# Dan Martell: books

Summaries in my own words from his public talks and interviews. No book text is reproduced.

## Buy Back Your Time: Get Unstuck, Reclaim Your Freedom, and Build Your Empire (2023)

- **Year:** 2023 (inferred from launch-period interviews S018 and S020, January 2023, and S062, 2022-09, where he describes finishing it). He calls it a New York Times bestseller (S015).
- **How it was made:** About two and a half to three years, with author Ron as "book CEO," an agent, researchers, and his video catalog mined for stories; he edited rather than wrote from scratch (S020, S041, S062, S126).
- **Core thesis:** Founders do not hire to grow the business; they hire to buy back their time. Entrepreneurs will not grow into pain, so at each pain line they stall, sell, or sabotage unless they systematically move low-value, draining work off their calendar and refill it with work that makes money and energizes them. The aim is an empire, a life you never need to retire from, not a four-hour workweek (S014, S017, S018, S020, S031).
- **Key frameworks as he teaches them in public:**
  - The Buyback Principle (calendar over capacity) and the pain line with the three S's (stall, sell, sabotage).
  - The Five Time Assassins (staller, speed demon, supervisor, saver, self-medicator).
  - The DRIP Matrix (delegate, replace, invest, produce).
  - The Buyback Rate (income divided by 2,000, divided by 4).
  - The Buyback Loop: time and energy audit, transfer, fill.
  - The Camcorder Method and playbooks; definition of done; 10-80-10.
  - The Replacement Ladder (admin, delivery, marketing, sales, leadership).
  - The Test-First hiring method.
  - The Perfect Week and the Preloaded Year (he says the Perfect Week is chapter eight, S019, and the book shows a diagram of his own week, S015).
  - Transformational leadership and the 1-3-1 rule; 50 to Fix It (he says he called it the "$50 magic pill" in the book, S014).
  - The 10x Vision Map (personal, business, empire, lifestyle).
- **Sources that teach it:** S014, S015, S016, S018, S019, S020, S031, S037, S038, S052, S057, S059, S061, S062, S108, S122.
