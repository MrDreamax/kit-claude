# Books: Gino Wickman

One entry per book. Summaries are in my own words from Gino's public teaching and official EOS pages. No book text is reproduced. Years are publication years from the public record; the corpus confirms co-authors where noted.

## Traction: Get a Grip on Your Business (2007; expanded edition 2011)
- **Thesis:** A 10 to 250 person entrepreneurial company can run "like a Swiss watch" (S026, S043) if it strengthens six components and runs on one simple operating system.
- **Key frameworks:** Six Key Components, V/TO and the Eight Questions, Accountability Chart, Right People Right Seats (GWC, People Analyzer), Rocks, Meeting Pulse and the Level 10 Meeting, Scorecard, IDS and the Issues List, the 3-Step Process Documenter, Hitting the Ceiling and the five leadership abilities.
- **Taught in:** S026, S035, S043, S064, S082, S117, S125, S126, and EOS material S001 to S024. Publishers rejected it 31 times before it found a home (S032, S082).

## Get a Grip (2012, with Mike Paton)
- **Thesis:** The same EOS model told as a business fable: a leadership team implementing EOS session by session, so readers see the process and the human resistance.
- **Key frameworks:** The EOS Process (Focus Day, Vision Building, Quarterly and Annual sessions), plus the Traction toolbox.
- **Taught in:** Co-author confirmed in S120 and S123. The corpus has little direct teaching of the fable itself.

## Rocket Fuel (2015, with Mark C. Winters)
- **Thesis:** Great entrepreneurial companies have two people at the top: a Visionary and an Integrator. The right match is rocket fuel; going without an Integrator is the most common reason founders stall.
- **Key frameworks:** Visionary and Integrator roles, the Visionary/Integrator match, when to hire or partner with an Integrator, equity versus salary for the Integrator, the 4-to-1 scarcity of Integrators.
- **Taught in:** S025, S026, S029, S035, S042, S055, S077, S082, S101, S102, S126, and EOS material S013 and S015.

## How to Be a Great Boss (2016, with René Boer)
- **Thesis:** Engagement comes down to having a great boss, and being one is a short list of practices, not a personality.
- **Key frameworks:** Quarterly Conversations, Delegate and Elevate for managers, the 80 percent rule for boss work (EOS material S014), the Three-Strike Rule, rewarding and recognizing people.
- **Taught in:** S115, S124, S127, and EOS material S014 and S023. Co-author confirmed in S127.

## What the Heck Is EOS? (2017, with Tom Bouwer)
- **Thesis:** EOS only sticks when every employee, not just the leadership team, understands and uses the tools.
- **Key frameworks:** One tier at a time; the five foundational tools (V/TO, Accountability Chart, Rocks, meetings, Scorecard) taught by each leader to their own people.
- **Taught in:** S036, S038, S086. Co-author confirmed in S127.

## Entrepreneurial Leap (2020)
- **Thesis:** Only a small share of people are born with the traits to build a company with employees. Confirm whether you are one before you leap, then choose the business you are built for and follow a path that avoids the common mistakes.
- **Key frameworks:** Six Essential Traits, the Entrepreneurial Range, Confirm/Glimpse/Path, the Eight Critical Mistakes, My Biz Match (industry, type, size), the Vision Clarifier, finding a mentor, 10-year thinking, the nine stages of building a business.
- **Taught in:** About 25 sources, the largest cluster in the corpus: S027, S032, S039, S041, S042, S044, S050, S052, S053, S055, S056, S060, S062, S063, S077 to S085, S118, S119.

## The EOS Life (2021)
- **Thesis:** The point of running a great business is an ideal life: doing what you love, with people you love, making a difference, compensated appropriately, with time for other passions.
- **Key frameworks:** The five points of the EOS Life, scoring each 1 to 10, the 10 disciplines for managing energy, extending the EOS Life to employees.
- **Taught in:** S025, S028, S029, S040, S096, S097.

## Shine (2024, with Rob Dube)
- **Thesis:** Driven entrepreneurs who win in the outer world often feel empty inside. Inner work, built on simple disciplines, lets you be driven and have peace.
- **Key frameworks:** The Three Discoveries (I am driven; all decisions are made out of love or fear; it is possible to be driven and have peace), the 10 Disciplines, the True Self Model, shedding layers.
- **Taught in:** S031, S037, S045, S046, S047, S048, S049, S057, S058, S076, S088, S089. Co-author confirmed in S088.

## Let It Fly: 15 Riffs for Driven People (with Rob Johnson)
- **Thesis:** Unscripted riffs for driven people on why success does not always feel like enough.
- **Taught in:** Listed on his site (S116). The corpus has no teaching of it; do not cite it for frameworks.
